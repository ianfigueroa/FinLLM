Item 1. Business
Acme Corp sells industrial analytics software to banks, insurers, and asset managers.
Subscription revenue increased in 2025 because enterprise renewals improved and more customers adopted cloud modules.

Item 1A. Risk Factors
Acme depends on a small number of large financial customers. Losing one of those customers could reduce revenue materially.
Acme also depends on cloud vendors, and outages or vendor price increases could affect margins.

Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis
Management attributed margin expansion to renewal pricing, lower hosting costs, and disciplined headcount growth.
